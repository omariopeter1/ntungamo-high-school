Our Story

More Than A School.

Ntungamo High School has been serving students since 1995. The school was started by the loving Rev. Fr. Bashoobora, whose vision helped create opportunities for many young people to pursue education.

Our school believes that every student deserves an opportunity to discover their potential. Whether rich or poor, naturally gifted or still discovering their abilities, every learner is part of our community.

Student Clubs

Find Your Community.

With more than ten clubs and activities, students have many opportunities to discover their interests and talents.

🔬 Science Club

The Science Club encourages students to experiment, innovate and share brilliant ideas. Under the guidance of the club patron, Mr. Matayo, students explore science and ICT while developing practical skills.

🎭 Drama Club

Drama and dance provide a space where students can express themselves, develop confidence, discover their talents and enjoy time together.

🎤 Debate Club

Debate helps students develop communication, confidence, critical thinking and the ability to express ideas clearly while respecting different opinions.
